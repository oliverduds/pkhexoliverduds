v3 design notes

- Uses PKHeX LegalityAnalysis as the source of truth.
- SameEncounterIdentity guard compares encounter type/species/form/version/generation/context/location/level range/egg/fixed ball.
- Master Ball is the only legal-but-conspicuous ball normalized automatically.
- ALM regeneration is last resort and all regenerated entries are explicitly reported.
- Final audit always runs.


v4.5:
- SAV7b PokeListHeader.STARTER / StorageSlotSource.Starter is treated as untouchable.
- LGPE party pointers are also preserved even though Party flags are not generic
  IsOverwriteProtected flags.
- PB7.IsStarter identifies Pikachu form 8 / Eevee form 1 for generator filtering.


v4.6:
- Do not use encounter-aware EvoCriteria.LevelMin as the Living Dex canonical
  evolution floor.
- Build the stage chain from EvolutionTree.Reverse.GetPreEvolutions.
- Resolve each edge from EvolutionTree.Forward.GetForward and propagate:
    max(previous + method.LevelUp, method.Level)
- CurrentLevel is allowed to increase when an earlier bad pass produced an
  under-level evolved species.
- Final audit separately checks canonical evolution-floor policy.


v4.7:
- For a direct evolved encounter, do NOT propagate the canonical chain from
  level 1 and clamp to MetLevel afterward.
- Instead propagate the entire normal chain FROM actual MetLevel:
    max(previous + method.LevelUp, method.Level)
- This distinguishes:
    Slowbro Met39 -> 40 (level-up)
    Arcanine Met11 -> 11 (item)
